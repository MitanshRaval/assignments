Session 2: How Software Works

1. What are the three main layers of software architecture?

1. UI (User Interface): The part of the software that users see and interact with.
2. Business Logic: Processes the user's actions and applies the rules and operations of the application.
3. Database: Stores and manages the application's data for later use.

2. Define client and server in your own words with one real-life example.

Client: A device or application that requests a service or information from another computer.

Server: A computer or system that receives requests from clients, processes them, and sends back the required information.

Example: When you open Instagram, the Instagram app on your phone is the client, while Instagram's servers process your request and send your feed back to your phone.

3. Write 2 differences each between desktop apps, web apps, and mobile apps.

Desktop Apps:
- Installed directly on a computer.
- Usually designed for Windows, macOS, or Linux.

Web Apps:
- Accessed through a web browser.
- Usually do not require installation on the user's device.

Mobile Apps:
- Designed for smartphones and tablets.
- Can use mobile features such as GPS, camera, and notifications.
